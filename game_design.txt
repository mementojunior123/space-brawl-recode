---Upgrades---
-Different upgrade types:
    -Minor (stat buff)
    -Major (stat buff)
    -Perk (passive that isn't just a stat buff)
    -Ability (activated manually, max 1, recharges at the end of the wave)
    -Specialization (Weapon upgrade / new weapon, max 1 weapon at a time)
    -One time (stackable upgrades that apply once)

-Each perk has a tier, from 1 to 10. This represents how strong it is relative to its type.
-As the game progresses, available upgrade tiers get higher. This makes upgrades scale with time.
    -You can only get upgrades near or at your current tier.
    -Upgrade rarity is determined by its individual weight, its type and its tier.
    -Certain upgrades can be obtained at any moment, irrespective of tier.
-Border shape and border color --> Upgrade tier/type
-Add messages in red for new weapons/abilities that replace other weapons/abilities
-The upgrade type and tier has to also be displayed somewhere else

-You can reroll the shop for new upgrades.
    -You can do it once fof free.
    -Every subsequent reroll increases the cost by 1 health. Ex : 1st reroll free, 2nd reroll 1 hp, 3rd reroll 2 hp...

-Specilization carryover:
    -When getting a new weapon, you will automatically get specilization of equivalent tier minus 1 level.
